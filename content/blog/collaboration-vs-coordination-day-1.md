Hello there! 👋

Here's a question worth sitting with:

Does your team *collaborate*, or do they *coordinate*?

Most teams coordinate. They divide the work. Each person takes a slice, heads to their corner, and types away. They sync up at standups. They integrate at pull requests. They ship when the pieces finally fit.

And that works. Sort of.

But coordinating teams work *around* the problem. Each person sees their slice. Nobody sees the whole.

Collaborating teams are different. They work *through* the problem together. Same room, same screen, same keyboard. One problem, multiple minds. The insight one person has becomes everyone's insight immediately.

It sounds slower. It's not.

When you coordinate, you pay the integration cost later. Merge conflicts. Misunderstood requirements. Rework.

When you collaborate, you pay it continuously. A few seconds here and there. No surprises at the end.

Coordination looks productive. Everyone's busy. Five tasks in progress.

Collaboration *is* productive. One task finished. Then the next.

Here's the thing: most teams think they're collaborating because they communicate. Slack messages, code reviews, meetings.

But communication isn't collaboration. Collaboration is inhabiting the problem together. Learning together. Building shared understanding in real time.

Your team might be great at coordination.

But are they collaborating?

Something to think about.

Yours,
