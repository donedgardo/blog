category: daily

Hello there! 👋

Nobody wants to look stupid.

We've all been there. Stuck on a bug. Trying random things. Googling the same error for the third time. Reading our own code like a stranger wrote it.

This is normal. This is programming.

But we hide it.

When someone walks by, we switch tabs. When we finally fix it, we commit like we knew all along. The struggle stays private. The result looks clean.

Now imagine doing that with someone watching. Sharing your screen. Thinking out loud. Wrong turns and all.

Uncomfortable, right?

That discomfort is why teams resist real collaboration.

Pair programming isn't just about two keyboards. It's about exposing the mess. Saying "I don't know" with a witness. Debugging in public.

And that vulnerability? It's not a bug. It's the feature.

When you struggle together, you learn together. The senior dev sees the junior's blind spots. The junior sees that the senior gets stuck too. Both get better.

When you struggle alone, you protect your ego. But you miss the learning.

Real collaboration requires safety. Safety to not know. Safety to be wrong. Safety to figure it out live, in front of someone else.

The mess is where the learning happens.

Let people see it.

Yours,
Ed
